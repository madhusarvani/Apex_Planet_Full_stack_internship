🚀 How to Set Up

Step 1: Visit the Setup Page

Open this URL in your browser:

http://localhost/task4/setup.html

Step 2: Click "Populate Menu Now"

The setup wizard will add all 18 food items to your database automatically.

Step 3: View Your Menu

Visit the customer menu page to see all your beautiful food items:

http://localhost/task4/customer/index.php
💡 Tip: The setup script is smart! It checks for duplicates and won't add items twice.
